# Calling on all workspace Admins - Connect your Bitbucket workspace to an Atlassian organization

July 7, 2025 edited

Update August 21, 2026

Hi all,

As of today, all Bitbucket workspaces on paid plan are eligible to be linked to an Atlassian Org of your choice. As a reminder, to perform the Org linking, the user must be an Admin of Bitbucket workspace and the Atlassian org.

---

Update May 11, 2026

Hi Bitbucket Community,

We’ve started linking Bitbucket workspaces on paid plans (Standard and Premium) to Atlassian organizations. If your workspace wasn’t already linked to an organization, we’re creating a new Atlassian organization for you and completing the linking automatically by choosing one of your Workspace Admins as an actor for this action.

This step sets the foundation for unified billing, centralized user management, and a consistent experience across Bitbucket and other Atlassian apps. Our goal is to give organization admins a single place to manage users, billing, and app access for all Atlassian apps.

If you’re a workspace admin, primary billing contact, or technical contact, you can access your new organization at admin.atlassian.com. The organization name matches your Bitbucket workspace ID (the name in your Bitbucket repository URLs).

Administrators can chance the name of the newly created Organization at any time, by following the steps outlined in this help document.

What to expect:

Audit logs: Workspace admins can find the linked organization ID in the Bitbucket workspace audit log. The audit log will reflect what user Atlassian team used as an actor. This is done to make sure that one of the workspaces Admins becomes an admin of a newly created Organization to be able to manage it.

Notifications: Users added as organization admins later, will receive an email notification below.

Next, we’ll begin migrating these workspaces to our new billing engine, followed by centralized user management towards the end of calendar year.

| Moving a Bitbucket app between organizations isn’t supported at this time. To follow progress on this feature, watch the following public request: https://jira.atlassian.com/browse/BCLOUD-23320 |
| --- |

---

Update as of November 3rd, 2025

Bitbucket is moving to a new billing platform and we are retiring our old billing system.

To keep your workspace running smoothly, all Bitbucket workspaces must be linked to an Atlassian organization by March 15, 2026.

What happens if you don’t link your workspace by the deadline?

If your workspace isn’t linked by March 15, 2026, Atlassian will create an organization for you and link your Bitbucket workspace to it. Your billing information, users, and groups will be migrated to this new organization. After that, you won’t be able to move your workspace to a different Atlassian organization.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hello Bitbucket Community,

We are significantly enhancing Bitbucket Cloud by deepening its integration with the Atlassian platform, providing our customers with a more unified experience across all Atlassian cloud products. Our primary goal is to enable administrators within your Atlassian organization to manage Bitbucket Cloud users, permissions, and billing alongside your other Atlassian flagship products, all directly within Atlassian Administration. Furthermore, this centralizes your organization's content to unlock enhanced benefits with Atlassian AI.

Who needs to take action?

If you’re an admin of a Bitbucket Cloud workspace, please link your workspace to your company’s Atlassian organization.

Note: To link your workspace to an Atlassian organization, you need to be a Bitbucket workspace admin and an Atlassian organization admin.

## Steps to link your workspace to an Atlassian Org

Please follow the steps in Add a new Atlassian app to your organization | Atlassian Support or use the steps provided below to complete the linking process.

#### If you are a Bitbucket workspace admin and an Atlassian organization admin

Go to admin.atlassian.com.

Select the organization you would want to link your workspace to. (Note: Please only select the workspaces that you want to be part of your organization as you will not be able unlink them later.)

Once inside the organization, select Apps

OR

Under Apps, click Atlassian apps

4. In the upper-right corner, click "..." and then select Link Bitbucket from the dropdown menu

5. Click Next on the screen

6. Select the workspace you want to link to your Atlassian organization. Note: You can only link one workspace at a time.

7. Confirm that this is the workspace you would want to link and click Link

8. Refresh the Apps view to see the newly added workspace on the list of available apps.

#### If you are a Bitbucket workspace admin but not an Atlassian organization admin

You will need to reach out to your organization’s admin.

Either ask them to make you an org admin, or follow the steps provided below to add them as a workspace admin to your workspace so they can complete the linking process.

Go to Workspace settings.

Select User groups.

Select the group with the Admin lozenge associated with it.

Select Add members in the upper-right corner.

Enter the user’s email (or multiple emails if sending to multiple administrators).

Select Confirm.

The user(s) will receive an invitation email to join the workspace as an admin, they must accept the invitation to join the workspace.

Once those steps are completed, they can follow the steps outlined in the “If you are a Bitbucket workspace admin and an Organization admin” section above to link your workspace to an Atlassian organization.

#### Not sure if you already have an Atlassian organization?

You can verify whether or not your company already has an Atlassian organization by going to https://admin.atlassian.com/.

1. If you are not part of any organization, you will see a prompt to create one

you can also follow the steps outlined in our help documentation.

3. When prompted, you can take a tour or close it

4. From the Atlassian Guard Standard screen, select a trial or click X to skip.

5. Once the org is created, follow the steps outlined in the section “If you are a Bitbucket workspace admin and an Organization admin” to link your workspace to an Atlassian organization.

Eligibility

Only workspaces that are eligible to be linked will appear in the list under ‘Select a workspace to link’.

If your workspace isn’t listed, it’s not yet eligible. Don’t worry—you’ll receive a notification when it becomes eligible.

FAQ

Is this step mandatory to continue using BB ?Linking your Bitbucket workspace to an Atlassian organization is required. Taking action now will also help you maintain uninterrupted access to the product and benefit from new upcoming enhancements. Deadline to complete the linking is March 15, 2026.

What happens if I don't link my workspace ?

If your workspace isn’t linked by March 15, 2026, Atlassian will create an organization for you and link your Bitbucket workspace to it. Your billing information, users, and groups will be migrated to this new organization. After that, you won’t be able to move your workspace to a different Atlassian organization.

If my company has multiple Atlassian Organizations, which Organization should I pick to link my workspace to?To best support centralized user and billing management across your Atlassian suite, we recommend selecting an Atlassian organization that already serves as your primary hub for flagship products like Jira and Confluence Cloud, and where you have existing integrations with Active Directory for user management. Consolidating your cloud products under a single Atlassian organization not only streamlines user and billing administration, but also unlocks advanced capabilities such as Atlassian AI, powered by the Atlassian Teamwork graph.Administrators should also consider active integration between Jira Cloud and Bitbucket workspace when making a decision about which Atlassian Org to choose. Consolidating all your cloud products under a single organizational umbrella will greatly simplify the daily responsibilities of administrators.

Will we lose any Bitbucket features or have impact on users access by linking our workspace to Atlassian Administration? No, no features or user access will be impacted by taking this step.

What is the role of Atlassian Organization Admin?Within Atlassian Organization, Admins have multiple roles, please refer to https://support.atlassian.com/user-management/docs/what-are-the-different-types-of-admin-roles/ to learn more.

What are the benefits from linking my workspace to an Atlassian Org?

Workspaces linked to an Atlassian org will be able to benefit from

Centralized user management

A unified billing experience. To learn more, please visit this blog post

Rovo Dev AI capabilities to streamline development

Set up Rovo Dev Agents

The upcoming Bitbucket Packages feature includes support for Docker images via a built-in container registry

This new feature will enable smooth storage, management, and deployment of containerized applications, all from within Bitbucket Cloud – avoiding the need for expensive third party solutions

Workspaces not linked to an Org will not be able to install Forge Apps

Hi there and appreciate your questions.

Here are the answers:

Does linking require that Bitbucket workspace admins also become Organization admins?If so, there is a concern that Bitbucket workspace administrators might be granted excessive privileges—such as permissions to configure organization-wide security policies or manage access to other Atlassian products—which may not align with their intended scope of responsibility.

In order to link your workspace to an Atlassian Org, the user must be an Org Admin and a workspace Admin. However that doesn't require then for the workspace Admins to become Org Admins. Atlassian Administration has a role of User Access Admin, this role allows users to manage product access but not have access to the product and doesn't require Org Admin role. To learn more please refer to this help document.

Is it possible to continue operating with separated user and admin management (i.e. without linking), even after the September 2, 2025 deadline?

By linking your workspace, you will still continue managing users separately. Please see my response above to Joe for more details.

If linking is mandatory, is there a way to restrict admin capabilities so that administrators can only manage users and settings related to the specific product (e.g. Bitbucket), without broader organization-level access?

Yes, please refer to the following help documents: https://support.atlassian.com/user-management/docs/what-are-the-different-types-of-admin-roles/

Thank you and please feel free to reach out to our Support team for help
