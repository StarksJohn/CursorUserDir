“For heavy duty tactical operations on the move, arms dealers can invest in a Mobile Operations Center (MOC), the kind of powerful support usually enjoyed only by trained assassins or rogue nuclear states. Storable in your Bunker, this drivable command center’s modular construction allows for tailored combinations of vehicle workshop, weapons workshop, luxury living quarters, and command center – with facilities for upgrading and customizing your arsenal and modifying Weaponized Vehicles (including turrets for your MOC that can be manned by your squad) – all at your fingertips, and pulled by a fully customizable commercial semi.”

—Rockstar [Newswire](http://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/60222/GTA-Online-Gunrunning-Underground-Bunkers-Mobile-Operations-Centers-an)

The [Pegasus](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Pegasus) [1] Mobile Operations Center is a modular semi-trailer featured in the [Gunrunning](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/GTA_Online:_Gunrunning) update for [Grand Theft Auto Online](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_Online).

## Design

#### Exterior

“You are the warzone.”

— [Warstock Cache & Carry](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Warstock_Cache_%26_Carry) description.

The Mobile Operations Center is depicted as a twin-axle, large-sized trailer with two stands towards the front, side extended walls with marker lights on the upper side, and side storage boxes. The rear guard rail, side ladders and tail lights are derived from the regular [Mule](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Mule), while the ramp door is actually derived from the ramp variant of the aforementioned vehicle. Unlike the Mule, however, the license plate is located on the center.

The trailer can be painted with two colors, where the primary one is applied on the entire body and the secondary one on the trim/outlines of the actual box (the part that excludes the side extensions). It uses industrial wheels derived from most industrial trucks, such as the [Benson](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Benson_(HD_Universe)) and [Phantom](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom).

The trailer can be [equipped](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicle_Features/Mounted_Weapons) with three cannon turrets, a large frontal one on the front wall of the trailer and two smaller turrets on the upper rear quarters of the body.

According to the files, the following vehicles can tow this trailer:

- [Hauler Custom](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Hauler_Custom)
- [Phantom Custom](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom_Custom)
- [Phantom Wedge](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom_Wedge)
- [Phantom](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom)

#### Interior

The MOC's cargo hold interior is large and is fitted with living quarters, a touch screen and other assets the player can purchase, assigned to three different bays. There is a secure door at the front and the rear ramp door for exit. A blue corona appears in front of the ramp door when stationary.

Although the trailer has a seating capacity for a limited number of players (depending of the assets installed in the bays), it has standing capacity for the entire lobby (depending on the owner's accessibility permissions).

## Performance

#### Vehicle

Taking control of such a large vehicle can be a challenge, as the third person perspective is rather obstructed by the sheer size of the MOC, and is difficult to measure the allocated width of the trailer in first person, especially in stretched areas. The extra width of the modules requires some care in avoiding immovable roadside obstacles, however the combined unit is able to withstand these impacts without detaching the trailer. The trailer height can cause some difficulty navigating low bridges, particularly freeway overbridges, although it can also clip through some obstacles such as the pedestrian walkway between the [Union Depository](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Union_Depository) and [Arcadius Business Center](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Arcadius_Business_Center).

Being a trailer, it cannot be driven, however it does feature its own handling line. The cab unit can be detached from the trailer and driven normally and re-attached at any time. Other semi-truck cabs can then be used to hook onto the MOC and tow it around, however they will not have the additional durability of the custom cabs.

Although difficult to perform, it is possible to crush motorcycles and small vehicles with the MOC by passing it over them, due to its high mass being 13.5 tonnes.

#### Armor

Although the size of the MOC will be an easy target for attackers, the trailer is very resistant to damage, taking 20 [RPGs](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/RPG_(HD_Universe)) or homing missiles to destroy it. The MOC cab is tied with the [Brickade 6x6](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Brickade_6x6) for the heaviest armoured vehicle in the game, being able to take 22 RPG rockets or 68 homing missiles (In comparison to the Brickade 6x6 that can withstand 47 RPGs or homing missiles). It can also bear the brunt of many high-damage attacks, such as shells fired from the [TM-02 Khanjali](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/TM-02_Khanjali), Flak Cannons from an [Anti-Aircraft Trailer](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-Aircraft_Trailer), the missiles of the [Kosatka](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Kosatka) and explosive cannons from various aircraft such as the [B-11 Strikeforce](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/B-11_Strikeforce) and the [P-996 LAZER](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/P-996_LAZER), making it a very safe refuge in the midst of a battle. As the cab is counted as a separate vehicle, destroying it would render the MOC static and vulnerable. If the MOC is destroyed, it will return to the bunker and can be respawned immediately, but that also means the armored cab must return to the bunker, even if it was not destroyed.

#### Weaponry

Gun Turret: depending of the upgrades, the trailer has access to either a single remote cannon turret on the front wall that fires high-velocity rockets, or two more on the rear quarters of the model. The cannon turrets behave similarly to those of the [Rhino Tank](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Rhino_(HD_Universe)) and the stock weapon of the [APC](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/APC), albeit with a higher fire rate (60 RPM), higher projectile velocity (1116 kph or 310 m/s) and longer range (1550m), as well as increased projectile lifetime (5 seconds). These weapons have a wide motion range that leave minimal blind spots around the trailer, with the frontal one covering approximately 45 degrees horizontal and 45 degrees vertical, and the rear ones approximately 120 degrees horizontal and 45 degrees vertical, making it an ideal defensive transport.

As a compromise of its firepower, however, is that occupants can only control one turret at once, meaning if there are less than three occupants and one decides to cover another blind spot, they would require to switch seats, which can be a lengthy process.

## Functionality

The MOC is added to the player's [Interaction Menu](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Interaction_Menu) and is able to be called to near* wherever the player is located and dispatched back to bunker storage on demand.

Inside the Command Center module is a touch-screen wall mounted display which gives the player access to eight [Mobile Operations](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Mobile_Operations) missions to unlock discounted prices on [Warstock Cache & Carry](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Warstock_Cache_%26_Carry) vehicles.

The Living Quarters serve only as recreation areas, with sofas, beds and chairs any player can sit on. A wardrobe is also available to change clothes, in the same way as most regular property wardrobes. It is available by default in Bay 1, although one can choose additional Living Quarters in Bays 2 and 3.

The Command Center option for Bay 1 adds three control terminals for the turrets. The left-hand side terminal controls the front turret (already included with the Command Center option), while the two right-hand side terminals control the rear turrets (which must be purchased as a modification for the MOC at the Bunker workshop).

The [Weapon Workshop](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Workshop) option for Bay 2 allows the player to upgrade selected weapons into [Mk II variants](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Mk_II_Weapons) and customize them with unique options. It also includes a Gun Locker that works similarly to the one found in [Offices](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Offices).

The Personal Vehicle Storage option for Bay 3 allows the player to store a single [Personal Vehicle](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Personal_Vehicles) of any type. The same vehicle can also be requested through the [Interaction Menu](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Interaction_Menu), under the "MOC services".

The Weapon & [Vehicle Workshop](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Weaponized_Vehicle_Workshop) option occupies Bays 2 and 3, and allows the player to access the same services as the Weapon Workshop option for Bay 2, and the ability to customize [Weaponized Vehicles](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Weaponized_Vehicles), as these cannot access to [Los Santos Customs](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Los_Santos_Customs) or [Beeker's Garage](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Beeker%27s_Garage). It should be noted that while the vehicle can be left stored there, it does not work as a save point and will reset back to its original garage when switching sessions or leaving the game.

Like the Brickade 6x6, Terrorbyte and several other vehicles, entering a MOC will instantly clear the player's wanted level, regardless of how many police are around.

## Customizations

The MOC's minimum base cost is $1,225,000 and for every non-default aspect, its price is increased, up to $2,790,000.

The player can modify the vehicle options at any time by re-visiting [Warstock Cache & Carry](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Warstock_Cache_%26_Carry) and re-specifying the customization options, but will not receive any credit for previously paid for options. Other modifications can be performed by the [Bunker Mechanic](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Bunker_Mechanic) inside the [Bunker](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Bunkers) at the Bunker [Vehicle Workshop](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Weaponized_Vehicle_Workshop).

With enough screens and lights to give anyone the impression that you know what you're doing, the Command Center bay is the choice of the professional. Comes with one exterior turret as standard.

Please note: passive mode is disabled while using the Mobile Operations Center with this bay installed.

A boutique arsenal and mod shop, this bay offers just the kind of privacy you need to craft those designer firearms you've only ever seen on the government black list.

Note: Includes gun locker and [Ammu-Nation](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Ammu-Nation_(HD_Universe)) functions.

## Prominent Appearances in Gameplay

- During [The Superdollar Deal](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/The_Superdollar_Deal/Finale), the player has to board the same MOC from the previous planning work in order to steal the printing plates. Two more are seen in the [NOOSE Headquarters](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/NOOSE_Headquarters) if the crew fails to intercept the truck, though they serve only as decorations.
- In [Planning Work: Tracking Device](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Planning_Work:_Tracking_Device), the player has to plant a tracking device on a MOC being held at [Fort Zancudo](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Zancudo) carrying printing plates.
- Enemy unarmed MOCs appear in the eighth and final Mobile Operations mission, [Work Dispute](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Work_Dispute), where two stationary ones need to be destroyed (before turned out to be decoys) and a third one carried by a Hauler Custom far away (after being revealed to be the actual objective).
- The MOC is the main objective for the fifth [Mobile Operations](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Mobile_Operations) mission [Cover Blown](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Cover_Blown), where the crew have to defend it with the three cannons while the driver retrieves it with a [Hauler Custom](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Hauler_Custom).

## Oversights

- Sometimes, players are able to see an NPC standing over the MOC, with their feet passing through the roof of the trailer. This particular NPC cannot be moved or killed by any means and sometimes, even the police will shoot at them. Even if the MOC is destroyed, the NPC will stay on its place, but will disappear along with the trailer when the message notifies that the MOC has been sent back to the bunker.

## Trivia

### General

The Mobile Operations Center's interior and purpose appears to take inspiration from the [F.L.A.G. Mobile Unit](https://knight-rider.fandom.com/wiki/F.L.A.G._Mobile_Unit) appearing in the 1982 TV series [Knight Rider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Rider_(1982_TV_series)). In the series, the interior features "high-tech" equipment, living quarters, interior storage, and a ramp allowing KITT to enter and be stored within the trailer, similar to the MOC.

- Furthermore, the [Phantom Custom](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Phantom_Custom), one of the semi trucks designed to tow the MOC, appears to resemble a [1984 GMC General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GMC_General), which is used to tow the aforementioned Mobile Unit in the TV series.

### Grand Theft Auto Online

None of the cannons are actually functional outside the trailer. Even if the gunner operates a cannon turret, anyone who is looking at them cannot tell the direction they are aiming.

- The front cannon may tilt sideways when performing sharp turns with a truck or when a player moves it by passing right over them.
