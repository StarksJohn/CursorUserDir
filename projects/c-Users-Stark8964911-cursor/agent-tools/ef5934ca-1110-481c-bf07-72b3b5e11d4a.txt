Quick Answer

ChatGPT pricing ranges from $0 (Free) to $200/month (Pro), with Business at $20–$25 per seat and Enterprise custom. The Free tier offers GPT-5.3 Instant with a 10-message-per-5-hours cap. Plus costs $20/month and includes GPT-5.5, Deep Research, Sora, and Agent Mode. Pro splits into two tiers: $100/month (5x Plus limits) and $200/month (20x limits, 1M-token context). Business requires a 2-seat minimum and starts at $20/seat on annual billing. API access is billed separately — GPT-5.5 costs $5.00 per million input tokens and $30.00 per million output tokens. Subscriptions include zero API credits.

Two years ago, ChatGPT cost either $0 or $20. Today there are several tiers, a separate pay-per-token API, and ads on the cheapest plans. The last few months alone have rewritten the pricing chart: GPT-5.5 launched April 23, 2026 and began rolling out across paid ChatGPT tiers. A new ~$100/month mid-tier was introduced in early April, alongside existing higher-tier plans. Business pricing now sits around $20–$25 per seat depending on billing. Ads have also been introduced on Free and Go plans in the U.S., though rollout is still limited and in testing.

For anyone evaluating ChatGPT for business, or just deciding if their $20/month Plus subscription still makes sense, the sticker price tells only half the story. The cost of ChatGPT at enterprise scale, where seat sprawl meets API usage meets model-generation cost increases, is a [FinOps](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/finops-tools/) problem that looks a lot like the early days of [cloud computing](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/what-is-the-cloud/).

This guide covers every ChatGPT pricing plan, real costs at scale, the full ChatGPT API pricing breakdown across every model in OpenAI’s lineup, and how GPT-5.5 changes the math. If you’re searching for OpenAI ChatGPT pricing, weighing the ChatGPT free vs. paid tradeoff, or building a business case for your CFO, every number here is verified against OpenAI’s published pricing.

## Free And Go: What $0 And $8 Actually Buy You

Does ChatGPT cost money? Technically, no. There’s a free tier. But the ChatGPT free tier limits tell a different story. Free users get GPT-5.3 Instant (not GPT-5.5) with a hard cap of 10 messages every five hours before the system either switches to GPT-5.3 Mini or asks you to wait. That ChatGPT message limit means one focused work session can exhaust your daily quota. Since February 9, 2026, the free plan also includes contextual ChatGPT ads in the US, OpenAI’s first step toward ad-subsidized AI.

Missing from Free: GPT-5.5, Sora video, Codex coding agent, Deep Research, Agent Mode, ChatGPT Images 2.0 Thinking Mode, Tasks, and any advanced reasoning. Is ChatGPT free? In the way that hotel lobby Wi-Fi is free. It exists, it works for checking email, and you’ll hit a wall the moment you try anything serious.

ChatGPT Go pricing sits at $8/month and launched globally in January 2026. The ChatGPT Go plan gives you 10x more messages, unlimited GPT-5.3 Instant, file uploads, and image creation. But ChatGPT Go still shows ads, still lacks GPT-5.5, and misses every feature that makes ChatGPT useful for professional work.

The ChatGPT Go vs. Plus gap isn’t about volume. It’s about capability. For $12 more per month (roughly the price of forgetting to cancel a free trial), ChatGPT Plus gives you the entire advanced feature suite.

Most practitioners CloudZero works with skip Go entirely. It’s OpenAI’s answer to price-sensitive markets, it launched in India at $4–$5 before going global, not a serious professional tool. As one cloud engineer put it: “Go is the plan you buy when you don’t know the other plans exist.”

## How Much Does ChatGPT Plus Cost — And Is It Still Worth It?

Moving from “should I pay at all” to “should I pay $20,” the calculus changes completely.

ChatGPT Plus costs $20/month with no annual discount — a price that hasn’t changed since February 2023, through three years and several major model upgrades. That $20 now includes GPT-5.5, Deep Research, Sora video, Codex, and Agent Mode. In dollar-per-capability terms, Plus has gotten dramatically better while the price held flat.

For most professionals, Plus is the tier where you get the full model suite without paying for headroom you don’t need.

What Plus doesn’t give you: GPT-5.5 Pro (the higher-accuracy variant — Pro tiers and above only), the 1M-token context window (Pro $200 only), Light and Heavy thinking-time toggles (Pro only), team admin tools, or automatic training-data exclusion. On Plus, your conversations may train OpenAI models unless you manually opt out. The Deep Research cap at 10 runs/month is the practical ceiling, heavy analysts burn through that in a busy week.

OpenAI has [publicly suggested](https://community.openai.com/t/why-raising-the-price-of-chatgpt-is-a-bad-idea-ethically-speaking/963194) ChatGPT Plus could reach $44/month by 2029 — more than double the current price. At $20/month, Plus remains one of the better value propositions in AI subscriptions, but multi-year budget models should account for the trajectory. The question isn’t if it’s worth it, it’s when you outgrow it.

## ChatGPT Pro Pricing: $100 Vs. $200 And Who Needs Which

When you do outgrow Plus, OpenAI now offers two options instead of one gut-wrenching jump to $200.

ChatGPT Pro starts at $100/month and goes to $200/month, splitting into two distinct tiers.

Pro $100 launched April 9, 2026, deliberately positioned against Anthropic’s Claude Max at the same price. It includes GPT-5.5, GPT-5.5 Pro, o1 Pro mode, and 5x Plus usage limits. Through May 31, 2026, a launch promotion doubles Codex usage to 10x Plus, dropping to 5x after. This is the realistic upgrade path from Plus — same model suite as $200, at half the price, with 5x instead of 20x limits.

Pro $200 gives you GPT-5.5 Pro with 20x Plus limits, 250 Deep Research runs/month, and roughly 1M tokens of context (~680 pages). It only makes sense if you exhaust the $100 tier daily — parallel workloads, heavy Codex usage, or large-context analysis requiring the full 1M-token window.

Is Pro worth it? The $100 tier answers this better than $200 ever did. It comes down to one question: do you hit limits? If yes, $100 is $80 well spent. If no, stay on Plus and invest the extra $80/month in something that doesn’t auto-renew, like actual coffee.

## ChatGPT Business and Enterprise: What Teams And Organizations Actually Pay

Individual plans are arithmetic. Organizational plans are where procurement headaches start, and where [FinOps for AI](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/finops-for-ai/) conversations become unavoidable.

ChatGPT Business pricing dropped from $25 to $20/seat/month (annual billing) on April 2, 2026. Monthly billing runs $25–$30/seat with a two-seat minimum. The ChatGPT Business plan includes everything in Plus, plus shared workspaces, SAML SSO, admin controls, SOC 2 Type II compliance, 60+ app integrations (Slack, Google Drive, Salesforce), and default training-data exclusion for business data.

At five or more users, ChatGPT Business costs the same as Plus — $100/month on annual billing — and adds SSO, SOC 2 compliance, admin controls, and default training-data exclusion. The crossover point where Business becomes the obvious choice is lower than most teams realize.

ChatGPT Enterprise pricing is custom, negotiated with OpenAI sales, and requires annual commitment. Industry reports suggest $60–$100+ per seat per month depending on volume and features. Enterprise adds SCIM, EKM, data residency in several regions, RBAC, domain verification, user analytics, audit logs, 24/7 priority support with SLAs, and custom legal terms. ChatGPT Enterprise pricing per user typically decreases with larger commitments.

But the ChatGPT Enterprise cost on your books is never just the per-seat rate. It’s seats plus API plus Codex credits plus model-generation cost increases every quarter. That’s the part most vendor conversations skip, and it’s why organizations managing AI at scale need [cloud cost management tools](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/cloud-cost-management-tools/) designed for this level of complexity.

## What GPT-5.5 Changes About ChatGPT Costs

GPT-5.5 deserves its own section because it fundamentally reshapes the cost conversation, and it’s the reason “ChatGPT pricing 2026” looks nothing like it did six months ago. For anyone asking “how much does ChatGPT cost per month,” the answer now depends on which model generation your plan or API defaults to.

GPT-5.5 pricing, also searched as ChatGPT 5 pricing, at $5.00 input / $30.00 output per million tokens is exactly 2x the GPT-5.4 rate. But zoom out eight months and the escalation is striking:

- August 2025: GPT-5 launches at $1.25/M input (standard tier)
- March 2026: GPT-5.4 arrives at $2.50/M input — 2x increase
- April 2026: GPT-5.5 ships at $5.00/M input — another 2x, just seven weeks later

GPT-5.5 input tokens cost 4x more than GPT-5 did at launch eight months ago — and half that increase happened in the seven weeks between GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5. During the same period, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang reported that [hardware costs per token dropped roughly 35x](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/nvidia-ceo-keynote-ai-inference-cost/). OpenAI’s pricing is moving in the opposite direction of its cost basis.

OpenAI’s margin expansion on the GPT-5 series is unprecedented in the API market. It’s the kind of pricing trend that makes [FinOps](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/finops-tools/) practitioners’ eyes twitch, and it’s why tracking [inference costs](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/inference-cost/) per model has become non-negotiable.

For developers, this means API costs roughly double with every model generation. If workloads auto-adopt the latest model, OpenAI’s default, your [AI costs](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/ai-costs/) double overnight with zero change in usage.

One important offset: Batch API and Flex processing both offer 50% off standard rates. At Batch pricing, GPT-5.5 costs $2.50/$15.00 per million tokens, identical to GPT-5.4 standard pricing. For offline workloads, the price doubling effectively disappears.

## 5 Hidden Costs Of ChatGPT That Most Pricing Guides Miss

Every competitor in the SERP gives you a plan-by-plan feature table. Useful, but it’s like reading the menu at a restaurant that charges for water, bread, and the privilege of sitting down.

### 1. API costs on top of subscriptions

Subscriptions and API are separate billing streams. An org paying $20/seat/month for Business AND building on the API pays both. A team of 10 on Business ($200/month in seats) running moderate GPT-5.5 API workloads can add $2,000–$5,000/month in token costs on top. Most pricing guides present subscriptions and API as either/or. They’re both.

### 2. Seat sprawl across tiers

Without governance, teams self-select. Marketing buys Plus. Engineering buys Pro $200. Finance stays on Free. The intern is on Go. The blended ChatGPT cost per month becomes impossible to track without centralized visibility.

### 3. The GPT-5.5 model cost escalation

Every model upgrade resets the cost baseline. If API workloads auto-adopt GPT-5.5, OpenAI’s default, costs double overnight with zero usage change. Organizations running multiple models need per-model cost tracking to avoid sticker shock.

### 4. Codex and agent overages

ChatGPT Codex pricing is tied to your plan tier, not billed per token like the API. Pro $100’s 10x Codex promotion expires May 31, 2026, then drops to 5x. After included limits, users buy credits, another line item that surprises finance teams quarterly.

### 5. No cost allocation by default

OpenAI billing doesn’t break costs by team, project, or feature. According to [CloudZero’s FinOps in the AI Era report](https://www.cloudzero.com/guide/finops-in-the-ai-era-2026-survey-report/), 78% of organizations bundle AI costs into general cloud spend, and only 20% can forecast AI spend within ±10% accuracy.

According to CloudZero data, [only 32% of companies allocate even half of [their OpenAI bill](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/track-openai-spend/). For orgs with 50+ seats, this is the AI equivalent of putting your entire cloud bill on a single credit card and hoping for the best.

## Frequently Asked Questions About ChatGPT Pricing

Does ChatGPT cost money or is it free?

ChatGPT has a free tier with strict ChatGPT message limits (10 per five hours), no GPT-5.5, and ads in the US. Paid ChatGPT plans start at $8/month (Go) and go to $200/month (Pro). ChatGPT Business pricing starts at $20/seat/month annual. Enterprise is custom.

How much does ChatGPT Plus cost per month?

ChatGPT Plus costs $20/month with no annual discount. It includes GPT-5.5, Deep Research (10/month), Sora, Codex, and Agent Mode. The price has held at $20/month since February 2023.

Is ChatGPT Pro worth $200 a month?

A $100/month Pro tier launched April 9, 2026 with the same model suite as $200 (including GPT-5.5 Pro) and 5x Plus limits. For most users who previously considered $200, the $100 tier is sufficient. Pro $200 justifies its premium only for daily power users who need 20x limits and 250 Deep Research runs/month.

What’s the difference between ChatGPT Plus and Pro?

ChatGPT Plus vs. Pro is a capacity question. Plus ($20/mo) gives GPT-5.5 with standard limits. ChatGPT Pro ($100 or $200) adds GPT-5.5 Pro, higher usage ceilings (5x or 20x Plus), and o1 Pro mode. ChatGPT Pro vs. Plus only matters if you’re regularly hitting limits.

How much does ChatGPT Enterprise cost per seat?

ChatGPT Enterprise pricing is custom, with reports suggesting $60–$100+/seat/month depending on volume and features. Enterprise requires annual commitment. ChatGPT Enterprise pricing per user typically decreases at larger seat counts.

Is ChatGPT API pricing included in the subscription?

No. ChatGPT subscription cost and ChatGPT API cost are separate billing streams. GPT-5.5 API: $5.00/M input, $30.00/M output. Business at $20/seat includes zero API credits.

How do I track ChatGPT costs across my organization?

OpenAI billing doesn’t break costs by team, project, or feature. CloudZero’s OpenAI integration provides granular cost allocation by model, team, customer, and feature — alongside cloud infrastructure spend.

Is ChatGPT Go worth it?

ChatGPT Go at $8/month removes some Free-tier limits but still shows ads and lacks GPT-5.5, Deep Research, Sora, Codex, and Agent Mode. Is ChatGPT Go worth it? For $12 more, Plus gives you the complete feature set. Most professionals skip Go.
